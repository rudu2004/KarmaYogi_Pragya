iGOT Karmayogi Enrollment

Follow these 3 easy steps to enroll on iGOT

1
Login to Parichay

Use your government email ID (@gov.in or @nic.in) to login to the Parichay portal.

2
Navigate to iGOT

Select the iGOT Karmayogi application from your Parichay dashboard.

3
Enroll in Course

Search for the recommended course and click 'Enroll' to begin your learning journey.
